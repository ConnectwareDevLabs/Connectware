3. CLOUDFLARE NATIVE CONTACT FORM & EMAIL ROUTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Handler: src/worker.js & functions/api/contact.js (/api/contact)
- Uses Cloudflare Workers Native Email Routing (`env.EMAIL.send()`).
- Cloudflare Dashboard Configuration Steps:
  1. Go to Dash > connectware.in > Email > Email Routing:
     * Enable Email Routing for the domain.
     * Under "Destination addresses", ensure "contactus@connectware.in" is
       verified.
  2. Under Workers & Pages > connectware > Settings > Bindings:
     * Ensure an "Email Routing" binding named "EMAIL" is connected
       (configured automatically via wrangler.jsonc "send_email").
- Security & spam protection included:
  * Built-in honeypot trap (_gotcha field) to block automated bot spam.
  * Server-side email format and required fields validation.
