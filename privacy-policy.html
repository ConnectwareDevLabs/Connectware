Privacy Policy.

Your trust is important to us. Learn how Connectware Solution safeguards your personal data.

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Connectware Solution PVT. LTD. ("Connectware", "we", "our", or "us"). We operate the website located at https://connectware.in. We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy and handling your personal information with full transparency, integrity, and diligence.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, interact with our contact forms, use our digital services, or view advertisements on our site.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information that you voluntarily provide to us, and information collected automatically when you navigate our website.

A. Information You Provide to Us

When you contact us, request a consultation, or submit an inquiry via our contact form, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and company name.
  • Project Details: The service of interest (such as Web Development, App Development, Digital Marketing, or SEO) and the message or inquiry contents.

B. Information Collected Automatically

When you browse our website, our servers and third-party tools may automatically log certain technical information, including:

  • Log and Usage Data: Your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, time stamps, and pages viewed.
  • Device Data: Screen resolution, device category (mobile, tablet, desktop), and general approximate geographical location (country/city level).
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: Cookies, web beacons, and local storage data (see Section 4 below).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect for legitimate business and operational purposes, including:

  • Responding to your inquiries and providing client support and quotations.
  • Delivering, maintaining, and improving our web development, mobile app, and digital marketing services.
  • Analyzing website traffic trends and user interaction to enhance site performance and user experience.
  • Serving advertisements through Google AdSense to support the maintenance of our free content and services.
  • Preventing fraudulent activities, unauthorized access, and maintaining security.
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to remember preferences, analyze web traffic, and personalize content. Our website uses both session cookies (which expire once you close your browser) and persistent cookies (which remain on your device until deleted).

Categories of Cookies We Use:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for you to browse the site and use its features securely.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g., Google Analytics / Google Tag Manager).
  • Advertising / Marketing Cookies: Used by Google AdSense and advertising networks to show relevant advertisements based on your browsing habits.

You can adjust your cookie choices at any time using our on-site Cookie Settings or through your browser settings.

5. Google AdSense & Advertising Partners

We use Google AdSense (Publisher ID: pub-7685297242917201) to serve advertisements on our website.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads on our site based on users' visits to our site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds Choices or Your Online Choices.

6. Google Analytics & Google Tag Manager

We utilize Google Tag Manager (Container ID: GTM-K7GTDFST) and Google Analytics to monitor and analyze web traffic. These services collect standard internet log information and visitor behavior details in an aggregated and anonymized format. For more information on Google's privacy practices, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.

7. Legal Grounds for Processing (GDPR & DPDP Act Compliance)

We process your personal information under the following lawful bases:

  • Consent: Where you have given explicit consent (e.g., accepting non-essential cookies or submitting the contact form).
  • Contractual Performance: To take necessary steps at your request prior to entering into a business contract.
  • Legitimate Interests: To operate our business securely, maintain IT infrastructure, and improve our services.
  • Legal Obligation: Where required by applicable laws or governmental authorities.

8. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors who provide hosting, email delivery, analytics, and infrastructure maintenance under confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by law, subpoena, or in response to valid requests by public authorities.
  • Business Protection: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Connectware Solution, our clients, or the public.

9. Data Security and Retention

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures (including SSL/TLS encryption, firewall protection, and restricted server access) to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

10. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the California Consumer Privacy Act, and the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request copies of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Object to or restrict the processing of your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your data to another organization or directly to you.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time where processing was based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contactus@connectware.in.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and digital services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such information.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
